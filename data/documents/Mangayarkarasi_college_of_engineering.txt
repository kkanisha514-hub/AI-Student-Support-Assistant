ABOUT THE COLLEGE

Mangayarkarasi College of Engineering (MCE) is an engineering institution located at Mangayarkarasi Nagar, Paravai, Madurai – 625402, Tamil Nadu, India.

The college is a private, self-financing, co-educational and non-minority engineering institution. It is operated by Mangayarkarasi Educational Trust.

The college is affiliated with Anna University and is approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), New Delhi.

The college provides undergraduate engineering education in various technical disciplines and focuses on developing students' technical knowledge, professional skills and overall personality.

ACADEMIC INFORMATION

Mangayarkarasi College of Engineering offers undergraduate engineering programmes in different branches.

The programmes offered by the college include:

1. B.Tech – Agricultural Engineering

2. B.E. – Computer Science and Engineering

3. B.E. – Computer Science and Engineering
   (Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning)

4. B.E. – Electronics and Communication Engineering

5. B.E. – Electrical and Electronics Engineering

6. B.E. – Mechanical Engineering

COMPUTER SCIENCE AND ENGINEERING

The Computer Science and Engineering programme focuses on computer programming, software development, data structures, algorithms, databases, operating systems, computer networks, artificial intelligence and other areas of computer science.

Students are trained in programming languages, software technologies and problem-solving skills.

COMPUTER SCIENCE AND ENGINEERING
ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE AND MACHINE LEARNING

The Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning programme focuses on artificial intelligence, machine learning, data analysis, deep learning and intelligent computing systems.

Students learn concepts related to machine learning algorithms, artificial intelligence, programming, data processing and modern computing technologies.

ELECTRONICS AND COMMUNICATION ENGINEERING

The Electronics and Communication Engineering programme focuses on electronic circuits, communication systems, digital electronics, embedded systems, signal processing and related technologies.

Students receive practical and theoretical training in electronics and communication engineering.

ELECTRICAL AND ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING

The Electrical and Electronics Engineering programme focuses on electrical systems, electrical machines, power systems, control systems, electronics and renewable energy technologies.

Students learn both theoretical concepts and practical applications related to electrical engineering.

MECHANICAL ENGINEERING

The Mechanical Engineering programme covers engineering mechanics, thermodynamics, manufacturing, machine design, engineering materials and other mechanical engineering subjects.

Students receive practical training through laboratories and engineering-related activities.

AGRICULTURAL ENGINEERING

The Agricultural Engineering programme combines engineering principles with agriculture.

The programme includes areas such as agricultural machinery, irrigation, farm equipment, agricultural processing and engineering technologies used in agriculture.
