Jayaraj Annapackiam CSI College of Engineering (JACSICE)
Location: Margoschis Nagar, Nazareth, Thoothukudi District, Tamil Nadu - 628617.
Established: 2001. The college is run by the CSI Thoothukudi-Nazareth Diocese, which
manages more than 350 educational institutions in the district. Motto: "Make
Technology Reachable and Affordable to All". The campus spans about 25 acres on the
Nazareth-Sathankulam highway, roughly 40-45 km from Tirunelveli and Thoothukudi by
road and rail.

[SOURCE: College Information, PAGE: 2]
Approvals and Affiliation:
JACSICE is approved by AICTE (All India Council for Technical Education), New Delhi,
and is affiliated to Anna University, Chennai. The institution is accredited by NAAC,
and the CSE programme is accredited by the National Board of Accreditation (NBA).
The college is ISO 9001-2008 certified.

[SOURCE: College Information, PAGE: 3]
Departments and Programmes Offered:
JACSICE offers full-time four-year B.E./B.Tech undergraduate engineering programmes,
including Computer Science and Engineering (CSE), Electronics and Communication
Engineering (ECE), Mechanical Engineering (MECH), and Information Technology (IT).
A postgraduate M.E. programme in Computer Science and Engineering is also offered.
Undergraduate admissions are primarily counseled through TNEA (Tamil Nadu Engineering
Admissions) based on qualifying marks in Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics at the
Higher Secondary level; postgraduate admissions consider TANCET scores.

[SOURCE: College Information, PAGE: 4]
Campus Facilities:
The college has a computerized central library with a large collection of books,
journals and magazines. Facilities include modern laboratories, hostel
accommodation, transport, sports facilities, and student clubs for co-curricular and
technical activities. A dedicated placement cell coordinates internships and campus
recruitment with industry partners.

[SOURCE: College Information, PAGE: 5]
Department of Computer Science and Engineering (CSE):
Started in 2001, the CSE department focuses on providing quality technical education
along with leadership development, encouraging students to take up projects with
real-world and community applications.

Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE):
Faculty specialize in areas such as Networking, Communications, DSP, Biomedical and
Speech Processing, Control and Instrumentation, VLSI, Image Processing, and Power
Systems/Power Electronics. The department regularly organizes conferences, workshops
and training programs.

Department of Information Technology (IT):
Focuses on software development, network management, cybersecurity, and database
administration, and regularly conducts seminars and workshops on emerging trends in
IT for students, faculty and industry participants.
