Frequently Asked Questions
COMMON USER QUESTIONS

Q: Where is Mangayarkarasi College of Engineering located?

A: Mangayarkarasi College of Engineering is located at Mangayarkarasi Nagar, Paravai, Madurai – 625402, Tamil Nadu, India.

Q: What is the TNEA counselling code of Mangayarkarasi College of Engineering?

A: The TNEA counselling code of Mangayarkarasi College of Engineering is 5536.

Q: Is Mangayarkarasi College of Engineering affiliated with Anna University?

A: Yes. The college is affiliated with Anna University.

Q: Is Mangayarkarasi College of Engineering approved by AICTE?

A: Yes. The institution is approved by AICTE, New Delhi.

Q: What courses are offered at Mangayarkarasi College of Engineering?

A: The college offers programmes including Agricultural Engineering, Computer Science and Engineering, Computer Science and Engineering with Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, Electronics and Communication Engineering, Electrical and Electronics Engineering, and Mechanical Engineering.

Q: Does the college offer Computer Science and Engineering?

A: Yes. The college offers B.E. Computer Science and Engineering.

Q: Does the college offer Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning?

A: Yes. The college offers B.E. Computer Science and Engineering with Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning.

Q: Does the college provide hostel facilities?

A: Hostel facilities are available. Students should contact the college for current hostel availability, fees and rules.

Q: Does the college provide transportation?

A: Yes. Transportation facilities are available. Current routes and timings should be confirmed with the college.

Q: What is the official website of Mangayarkarasi College of Engineering?

A: The official website is https://mce-madurai.ac.in/

Q: How do I apply for a bonafide certificate?
A: Submit a request through the Student Portal under "Certificate Requests".
Certificates are typically issued within 3 working days.

Q: How can I contact my department office?
A: Each department office is located on the respective department floor in the
Academic Block. Office hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM on working days.

Q: What should I do if I lose my ID card?
A: Report the loss immediately to the Student Affairs Office and apply for a
duplicate ID card by paying the applicable fee.

Q: Is hostel accommodation available for all students?
A: Hostel accommodation is available on a first-come-first-served basis and is
allotted through the Hostel Office at the start of the academic year.
