JACSICE is affiliated to Anna University, Chennai, so all university-level
examinations (end-semester theory exams for B.E./B.Tech/M.E. programmes) follow
Anna University's Controller of Examinations (COE) schedule, not a college-only
timetable.

Where to check the official exam timetable:
- Official COE portal: aucoe.annauniv.edu (also mirrored at coe1.annauniv.edu)
- Official university site: www.annauniv.edu
Students should select "Affiliated Colleges" (as opposed to "University Departments"),
then choose the correct Regulation (e.g. Regulation 2021 or Regulation 2025) and
department to find the subject-wise date sheet PDF for the current semester.

[SOURCE: Anna University Examination Info, PAGE: 2]
General Examination Pattern (per Anna University norms):
- Theory examinations are typically of 3 hours duration, for a maximum of 100 marks.
- Exams are conducted in two sessions: Forenoon (FN), usually 10:00 AM to 1:00 PM,
  and Afternoon (AN), usually 2:00 PM to 5:00 PM.
- A short reading time (commonly around 10 minutes) is generally provided before the
  exam start time.
- Internal Assessment marks are based on periodic tests (commonly referred to as
  CA-I, CA-II, CA-III) conducted by the college, combined with the university's
  end-semester exam marks for the final grade.

[SOURCE: Anna University Examination Info, PAGE: 3]
Students with standing arrears should check the separate "Arrear Examination"
timetable, published alongside the regular semester schedule on the COE portal.
Regulation matters: students admitted under different regulations (e.g. Regulation
2021 vs. Regulation 2025) may have different subject codes and slightly different
exam schedules, so always download the timetable matching your specific regulation
and department.

Hall tickets must be downloaded and printed from the student login portal; carrying
a digital copy on a phone is not accepted inside the examination hall.

IMPORTANT NOTE FOR ADMIN: Exact subject-wise exam dates change every semester and
are published by Anna University as official PDFs closer to each exam session. This
document intentionally does NOT list specific dates to avoid giving students
outdated or incorrect information. When each semester's timetable PDF is released,
download it from aucoe.annauniv.edu and upload it here via the admin upload feature
(or add specific rows to the `exam_schedule` SQLite table) so the assistant can give
exact dates for that semester.
