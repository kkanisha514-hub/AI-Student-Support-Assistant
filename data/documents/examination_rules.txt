Greenfield Institute of Technology (DEMO DATA)
Examination Rules and Guidelines

1. Students must carry their college ID card to every examination. Entry without
   an ID card is not permitted.
2. Students must report to the examination hall at least 15 minutes before the
   scheduled start time.
3. Electronic devices, including mobile phones and smartwatches, are strictly
   prohibited inside the examination hall.

[SOURCE: Examination Rules, PAGE: 2]
4. Any student found using unfair means during an examination will have their
   answer script confiscated and will be reported to the Controller of
   Examinations for disciplinary action.
5. Students requiring a revaluation must submit the revaluation form within
   7 days of result declaration, along with the prescribed fee.
