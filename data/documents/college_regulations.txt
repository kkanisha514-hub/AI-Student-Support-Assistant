Greenfield Institute of Technology (DEMO DATA)
General Academic Regulations

Attendance Requirement:
Every student must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in each subject during a
semester to be eligible to appear for the end-semester examination. Students with
attendance between 65% and 74% may be granted condonation only on valid medical
grounds, subject to approval by the Head of Department.

[SOURCE: College Regulations, PAGE: 2]
Grading System:
The college follows a 10-point relative grading system. Grades range from O
(Outstanding) to F (Fail). A minimum of grade D is required to pass a subject.
Students who fail a subject must reappear in the supplementary examination
conducted at the end of the semester.

[SOURCE: College Regulations, PAGE: 3]
Code of Conduct:
Students are expected to maintain discipline on campus. Ragging in any form is
strictly prohibited and will lead to immediate suspension. Use of mobile phones
during examinations is strictly prohibited and will be treated as a case of
malpractice.
